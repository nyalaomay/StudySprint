Your Practice Questions

Topic: Newton's Laws of Motion

1. What does Newton's First Law state?

An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion at constant velocity, unless acted on by an unbalanced force.

2. Write the formula for Newton's Second Law.

F = ma — force equals mass times acceleration.

3. Give a real-world example of Newton's Third Law.

When you push against a wall, the wall pushes back against you with equal and opposite force.
