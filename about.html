About StudySprint

StudySprint helps students turn class notes into quick practice quizzes using generative AI. It's built for peer-led study — Supplemental Instruction leaders and tutors can generate extra practice material in seconds instead of writing it from scratch.

How it works

  1. Paste your notes or type a topic on the New Session page.
  2. StudySprint sends your input to an AI model (Groq) on the backend.
  3. The AI generates a set of practice questions with explanations.
  4. Your session is saved so you can revisit it anytime on the History page.
